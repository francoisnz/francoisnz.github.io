Why We Decided to Transition

Residential setups maximize value when matching generation with daytime workloads. By re-routing our thermal and heavy appliance operations to peak daytime cycles, we optimized structural self-reliance without immediately overbuilding storage tiers.

This layout accommodates localized environmental factors, roof angles, and shade boundaries carefully evaluated before anchor deployments.
